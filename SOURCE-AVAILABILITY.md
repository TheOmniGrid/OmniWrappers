# Source availability

The private OmniWrappers installer source is not hosted in this documentation
repository. That choice does not remove source obligations or recipient rights
for covered third-party components.

- **DXVK basis:** marked OmniDXVK modifications are maintained privately; the
  zlib license permits binary redistribution without a source-delivery mandate.
- **dxwrapper / MemoryModule:** MPL 2.0-covered source corresponding to the
  delivered binary is available by reasonable means from `omnivex@theomnigrid.biz`, at
  no more than the cost of distribution, in addition to identified upstream source.
- **vkd3d-proton 3.0.1:** corresponding source and recursive submodules are
  identified by the `v3.0.1` tag. Recipients may request the exact corresponding
  source package from `omnivex@theomnigrid.biz` under LGPL 2.1.
- **dgVoodoo2:** not distributed inside OmniVoodoo2; obtain the official package
  and applicable terms directly from the
  [official upstream release](https://github.com/dege-diosg/dgVoodoo2/releases/tag/v2.87.3).

Source requests should name the OmniWrappers product, version, installer SHA-256 and
third-party component. Keep this offer accessible for the period required by
the applicable license and local law.
