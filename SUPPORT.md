# Support

Before contacting support:

1. Roll back any failed installation.
2. Confirm the exact real game EXE and whether it is 32- or 64-bit.
3. Record the selected OmniWrappers product and DLL names.
4. Use **Create support package** after selecting the game.
5. Do not send game files, account tokens, personal documents or crash dumps
   unless support explicitly explains why they are needed.

Contact: `omnivex@theomnigrid.biz`

Support is best-effort. Compatibility depends on third-party games, drivers and
protection systems that OmniWrappers does not control.
